Account deletion

Delete your InvTrack account and data

App: InvTrack (also shown as InvTracker in some older versions; Google Play package com.invtracker.inv_tracker) • Developer: Ravi Teja Kamalapuram • Last updated: October 10, 2026
In short: this page is the web account-deletion option for InvTrack on Google Play. In the app, open Settings > Data & Account > Delete Account. If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email support@invtracker.app from the email address of your account. Deleting in the app usually finishes straight away; anything that cannot finish there is completed within 7 days.

1. Delete your account in the app

You can export your data first: Settings > Data & Account > Export as CSV or Export as ZIP. Deleting an account cannot be undone once it has finished.

  1. Open InvTrack and go to Settings > Data & Account.
  2. In the Danger Zone section, tap Delete Account. If you use InvTrack as a guest, look for Delete Guest Data instead (see Guest accounts).
  3. A Delete Account dialog lists what will be deleted. You can tap Export a backup first, Cancel, or Delete Everything.
  4. A second dialog asks you to type DELETE. Type it, then tap Delete My Account.
  5. If you signed in with Google more than about 4 minutes ago, InvTrack asks you to sign in with Google again before it deletes anything. If you cancel that sign-in, nothing is deleted. If the sign-in fails for another reason, your request is kept and the account is deleted within 7 days (see How long it takes).
  6. When the deletion finishes, the app shows Account deleted successfully.

2. Delete your account without the app

Use this if you have uninstalled InvTrack or cannot sign in.

  1. Write to support@invtracker.app from the email address of the account, which is the email you sign in with (your Google sign-in email).
  2. Suggested subject: InvTrack account deletion request.
  3. You do not need to send anything else. You are not asked for anything beyond writing from that email address.

The developer, Ravi Teja Kamalapuram, looks up the account by that email address and files the same deletion request the app would have filed. The request is then handled as described in How long it takes. Nothing is deleted at the moment the request is filed; deletion starts after the 24-hour waiting period.

3. How long it takes

  • In the app, in the normal case: if you are online and your sign-in is recent, deletion is immediate. The app files the request, deletes your cloud data and your sign-in account, and shows Account deleted successfully.
  • If the app cannot finish (for example you are offline, the Google sign-in fails or deleting the cloud data fails), your request is kept. The app tells you that your account is scheduled for deletion and will be fully deleted within 7 days, that some of your data may already be gone, and that to keep your account you should sign in again within 24 hours and withdraw the request.
  • Requests filed by email are handled the same way as requests the app could not finish. The 7 days are counted from when the request is filed.
  • The 24-hour waiting period: the deletion job only handles requests older than 24 hours, so you have time to change your mind. If you sign in to the app again in that time, you will see Account scheduled for deletion with a Withdraw request button.

4. What is deleted

  • Your cloud data: your investments, cash flows, expected payments, goals, the details of files you attached (not the files themselves, which are on your device), health score history, FIRE settings, profile preferences, cached exchange rates and saved custom investment labels, including anything you archived, and then your account record.
  • Your sign-in account: the Firebase Authentication user for your account.
  • On your device: the files you attached, any saved guest backups for this account, and the app preferences stored for your account. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes anything else stored on the device.
  • Google Analytics events tied to your user ID: we ask Google Analytics to delete them. Google carries this out on its own schedule.
  • The deletion request itself: removed last, after the deletion has been checked.

5. What is kept, and for how long

WhatDetailsHow long
Deletion record A record that the deletion happened: a 16-character one-way hash of your account ID (not the ID itself, and no name or email), where the request came from, when it was requested, how many records were deleted, whether your sign-in account was deleted, whether the deletion was checked, the result of the Google Analytics deletion request, the outcome and when it finished. Kept as a deletion record. No retention period has been set for these records yet.
Crash reports already sent (Firebase Crashlytics) We cannot delete individual crash reports. Once you are signed out or your account is deleted, new crash and analytics reports from the device no longer carry your user ID. Deleted automatically at the end of Google's Crashlytics retention period.
Google Analytics events Events tied to your user ID, until Google processes our deletion request. Kept by Google for the retention period set in Google Analytics, until the request is processed on Google's own schedule.
Files you exported or shared CSV or ZIP exports and anything you sent through the share sheet stay where you put them. Until you delete them yourself.
Emails to support Including an account deletion request sent by email. As long as needed to answer you.

6. Guest accounts

If you use InvTrack as a guest, use Delete Guest Data in Settings > Data & Account before you uninstall the app. A guest account has no email address, so if you have already uninstalled the app we cannot tell which account is yours, and its data cannot be recovered.

7. Questions

Write to support@invtracker.app. Read how InvTrack handles your data in the InvTrack Privacy Policy.
