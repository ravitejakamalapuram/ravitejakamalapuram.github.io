Core Privacy Principles Across All Extensions

  • Local-First Architecture: All data processing, transformation, computation, and storage occurs entirely within your local browser.
  • Zero Remote Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, profile, or sell any personal data, browsing history, keystrokes, or IP addresses.
  • No Remote Tracking or Analytics: We do not embed any third-party tracking pixels, Google Analytics, telemetry SDKs, or advertising scripts.
  • Immediate Deletion: Uninstalling any extension instantly purges all local storage and preferences from your device.
