1. Who we are

InvTracker ("the App", "we", "us") is an app for tracking alternative investments such as fixed deposits, P2P lending, bonds and similar holdings. It is developed and operated by Ravi Teja Kamalapuram, an individual developer, who is responsible for your personal data under this policy (the "data controller" under the GDPR and the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Contact: raviteja369.k@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account's name, email address, profile photo URL and a unique user ID. The App only asks Google for basic sign-in. It does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Sheets or contacts.
  • Guest mode: if you choose "Continue as Guest", Firebase creates an anonymous account with a random user ID. No name or email is collected. You can later link a guest account to Google and keep your data.

2.2 Financial and planning data you enter

Everything you type into the App is saved to your private cloud account (see section 4), including:

  • Investments: name, type, status, platform, notes, dates (start, maturity, closed), expected rate, tenure, payout and compounding settings, risk level, currency.
  • Cash flows: date, type (invested, returned, income, fee and so on), amount, currency and notes.
  • Expected / projected payments: expected date and amount, and matched actual payments.
  • Goals: name, target amount or monthly income, target date, linked investments.
  • FIRE (financial independence) settings: monthly expenses, your current age, target retirement age, life expectancy, inflation and return assumptions, expected pension and passive income.
  • Portfolio Health Score history: the scores the App calculates for you over time.
  • Profile preferences: preferred currency, language, country code (derived from your device locale, not GPS) and date format.
  • Cached exchange rates used for currency conversion.

2.3 Documents and photos you attach

You can attach files (for example a deposit receipt) by picking a file or taking a photo. The file itself stays on your device in the App's private storage and is not uploaded to our servers. Only the document's details (display name, file name, type, size and the on-device path) are synced to your cloud account so the App can list it. Because the file stays on the device, attachments do not follow you to a new phone unless you use the App's export/import feature. Camera access is only used when you choose to take a photo.

2.4 Usage, device and diagnostic data (collected automatically)

  • App usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): screens viewed and feature events (for example "investment created", "goal created", "report exported"), with coarse context such as investment type or an amount range bucket. We do not send exact amounts, investment names or notes to analytics. Analytics also collects device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate region (derived by Google from your IP address), app-instance and advertising identifiers, and your InvTracker user ID.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crash and error details, stack traces, device state (model, OS, memory, orientation) and your InvTracker user ID so we can fix bugs.
  • Performance data (Firebase Performance Monitoring): app start times, screen rendering and network request timings, with device and app information.

2.5 Data kept only on your device

  • App preferences (theme, privacy mode, notification settings).
  • Your app-lock PIN, stored only as a salted, one-way hash in the device's secure storage. Biometric unlock (fingerprint/face) is handled entirely by Android; we never receive biometric data.
  • An offline copy of your synced data, kept by Firebase so the App works without internet.
  • Scheduled reminders (for example maturity or income reminders) are created as local notifications on your device. We do not use push-notification servers.

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your bank or brokerage login details, card numbers, PAN/Aadhaar, precise (GPS) location, contacts, SMS or call logs. We do not read your other files; you choose each file to attach or import.

4. Where your data is stored

Your account and records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. Each user's records are stored under their own user ID, and database security rules allow only the signed-in owner of that ID to read or write them.

Google Cloud data centres may be located outside your country, including outside India and the EU. Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance data is processed by Google, mainly in the United States. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, which include standard contractual clauses for international transfers.

The App also keeps an offline copy of your data and your attached files in its private storage on your phone. Android device backup is turned off for the App, so this local data is not copied into your Google device backups.

5. Service providers and third parties

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Functions): hosting, sign-in, sync, analytics and diagnostics. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In: signing in with your Google account. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Exchange rate services (Frankfurter, with ExchangeRate-API as a fallback): the App asks for exchange rates by sending only currency codes and dates. No account or financial data is sent, although, as with any internet request, these services see your device's IP address.
  • Google Fonts: the App may download display fonts from Google's font servers, which see your IP address.
  • Google Play: in-app update checks use Google Play's update service.

Advertising: the App does not currently show ads, and no advertising network receives your data. If we ever add ads, we will update this policy and ask for any consent the law requires first.

Your own sharing: when you use Export (a ZIP of CSV files and your attachments) or share a report, the file goes wherever you choose (email, Drive, messaging app). Those apps' own policies apply.

We may disclose data if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users or the App.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account and financial records: kept for as long as your account exists. They are removed when you delete them or delete your account (section 8).
  • Guest (anonymous) accounts: kept until you delete them or link them to Google. Guest accounts that have been inactive for 30 days or more may be deleted automatically, together with their data. A guest account cannot be recovered after you uninstall the App or sign out, so export or link your data to Google if you want to keep it.
  • Analytics data: kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics (no longer than 14 months for user-level event data), then deleted or aggregated.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for about 90 days.
  • Performance data: kept for a limited period set by Google.
  • Data on your device: stays until you delete it in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Support emails: kept for as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.

After deletion, copies may remain in Google's encrypted backups for a short period before they are permanently erased under Google's own deletion schedule.

7. Security

  • All data sent between the App and Google Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The App blocks unencrypted (cleartext) network traffic.
  • Google encrypts data at rest on its servers.
  • Security rules make sure each user can only read and write their own records.
  • Optional app lock with PIN and/or biometrics, and a Privacy Mode that hides amounts on screen.
  • Analytics and crash reporting are designed not to include exact amounts or your notes.

Please note: your records are not end-to-end encrypted. As the operator of the Firebase project, the developer has administrative access to the database. We do not look at individual users' records except when you ask us to help with a problem or when the law requires it. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the App: open Settings → Data & Account → Delete Account, confirm, and type DELETE. This deletes your investments, cash flows, goals, FIRE settings, health score history, profile and cached exchange rates from our database, and deletes your sign-in account. Guest users can also use Delete Guest Data on the same screen. You may be asked to sign in with Google again to confirm.

Without the App: if you have uninstalled the App or cannot sign in, use the web deletion page: sign in with the Google account you used, and request deletion. We delete your account and all associated data within 7 days of your request, and you can withdraw the request during the first 24 hours. Guest accounts cannot be identified from outside the App; delete a guest account in the App (Delete Guest Data) before you uninstall it.

If you cannot sign in with Google: email raviteja369.k@gmail.com with the subject "InvTracker account deletion request" from the email address of the Google account you used. We will confirm it is you and delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

Files on your device: attached files and the offline copy on your phone are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Analytics and crash data is linked only to a random user ID and is deleted according to the retention periods in section 6.

  • Deletion: as described in section 8.
  • Objection or restriction: object to analytics processing or ask us to restrict processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complaint: complain to us first, and to a data protection authority if you are not satisfied.

To use any of these rights, email raviteja369.k@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days and may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner.
