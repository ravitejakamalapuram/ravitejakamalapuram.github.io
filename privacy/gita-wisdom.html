Privacy Policy for Gita Wisdom New Tab

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 | Publisher: Raviteja Kamalapuram
Summary: Gita Wisdom New Tab is a tranquil spiritual new tab replacement. All verse selections, translations, audio recitation files, and bookmark preferences are processed and stored locally on your device without any external network tracking.

1. Single Purpose

Gita Wisdom New Tab replaces the default new tab page with a tranquil spiritual dashboard providing daily Bhagavad Gita shlokas, audio recitation, and multilingual translations entirely processed locally.

2. Zero Data Collection Policy

We believe your spiritual study and mindfulness practice should be completely private:

  • No Personal Information: No user identification, email address, or accounts required.
  • No Browsing History Access: The extension never reads or tracks URLs or web pages you visit.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: Zero third-party telemetry, ads, or tracking scripts.
  • Local Audio Playback: Audio chanting files are bundled or cached locally on device.

3. Permissions and Justifications

  • storage: Used solely to save user theme preferences (dark/light), font sizes, and bookmarked favorite verses locally via chrome.storage.local.
