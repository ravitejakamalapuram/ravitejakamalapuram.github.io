Privacy Policy for Telugu New Tab Calendar (పంచాంగం)

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 | Publisher: Raviteja Kamalapuram
Summary: Telugu New Tab Calendar (పంచాంగం) is a local calendar dashboard. All astronomical calculations, panchangam timings, and festival dates are calculated locally on your device without any external network calls or tracking.

1. Single Purpose

Telugu New Tab Calendar replaces the new tab page with a daily Telugu calendar and Panchangam dashboard calculated locally.

2. Zero Data Collection

  • No Personal Information: No login, identification, or registration required.
  • No Tracking: No third-party analytics, ads, or tracking cookies.
  • Local Processing: All tithi, nakshatra, and rahu kalam calculations run offline in browser JavaScript.

3. Permissions and Justifications

  • storage: Required to persist user location settings (time zone and city offset) locally on device via chrome.storage.local.
