Booking Flow Runner

Core Privacy Guarantee: Booking Flow Runner runs workflow execution automation strictly inside your local browser instance. No personal credentials, payment data, or browsing records are collected or transmitted.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Booking Flow Runner browser extension ("the Extension") handles data. We are committed to safeguarding user privacy and operating with total transparency.

2. Data Collection and Local Execution

The Extension executes pre-configured booking automation workflows on the target domain. All workflow executions, form inputs, and timing telemetry are evaluated locally in the user's browser. We do NOT collect, harvest, store remotely, or monetize any user personal data, credentials, or session cookies.

3. Permissions Disclosures

  • storage: Used solely to persist local execution preferences and cached workflow definitions on your device.
  • tabs: Required to verify tab state and coordinate step progression during flow automation.
  • Host Permissions: Used to inject automation steps on the target booking domain and retrieve official public workflow configurations from GitHub raw content.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Analytics

The Extension contains zero third-party telemetry libraries, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers.
