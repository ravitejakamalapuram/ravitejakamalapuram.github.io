InvTrack

An Android app for investments that have no daily price

Track what you invested and what came back. Fixed deposits, P2P lending, bonds, chit funds and gold, with your true annual return (XIRR) worked out for you.

The investments your broker app can't see

InvTrack is a ledger, not a price feed. You record what you put in and what came back (returns, interest, fees). It does not fetch market prices.

  • Fixed deposits
  • P2P lending
  • Bonds, NCDs and debentures
  • Chit funds
  • Gold, including Sovereign Gold Bonds
  • Real estate
  • Invoice discounting
  • Private equity and angel investments
  • Crypto
  • Anything else where money goes in and comes back later

Annual returns, worked out for you

A simple percentage ignores time. InvTrack uses XIRR, the annual-return formula behind Excel's XIRR, so the date of every payment counts.

Example: you put ₹1,00,000 into an FD on 1 April 2025 and get ₹1,07,100 back on 1 April 2026. That is 7.1% a year. Get the same ₹1,07,100 back on 1 October 2025 instead, and it is about 14.7% a year, because your money did the same work in half the time.

You also see total invested, total returned, net profit and how many times your money has multiplied (MOIC).

What you get

  • Never miss a payout

    • Reminders 7 days and 1 day before an investment matures.
    • Reminders when a regular payout is due, based on the payout schedule you set.
    • Turn each kind of reminder on or off in Settings.

  • Goals and FIRE in today's rupees

    • Set savings goals and track your progress.
    • Work out your FIRE number from your monthly expenses, with inflation built in, so the target is in today's money.
    • See how far your portfolio has got you.

  • Made for India, works globally

    • Amounts in lakh and crore (₹1,00,000) when your base currency is INR.
    • Record each investment in its own currency: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD and more.
    • Totals are converted to your base currency.

  • Data in and out

    • Add many investments at once from a CSV file, using the template in the app.
    • Export your whole portfolio as a ZIP file of CSVs, and import it on another device.
    • Attach PDFs and photos, such as FD receipts and statements, to an investment.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • Where your data lives

    • Your records are stored in InvTrack's Google Firebase project, under your account, so they sync across your devices. Google encrypts them in transit and at rest.
    • Works offline after first sign-in. Changes sync when you are back online.
    • Attached files stay on your phone.
    • We do not sell your data, and the app shows no ads.
    • The developer can technically access stored data. That happens only to answer a support request from you, to process a deletion request, or when the law requires it.

  • What is collected automatically

    • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring measure app usage and diagnose crashes and slowness.
    • This data is tied to your user ID and device identifiers. It is always on: there is currently no opt-out.
    • Amounts are reported only in ranges, not exact values.
    • InvTrack does not read your bank account or your messages. You enter your own data.

  • On your screen

    • Lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint.
    • Privacy mode hides amounts on screen with one tap.

  • Your control

    • Export your data any time: Settings > Data & Account > Export as CSV or Export as ZIP.
    • Delete your account and data in the app: Settings > Data & Account > Delete Account.
    • No app? Delete your account from the web.
    • Read the full privacy policy.

What InvTrack is not

  • Not a broker. You cannot buy or sell anything in the app.
  • Not an investment adviser. It shows your numbers; it does not tell you what to buy.
  • No bank or SMS access. You enter your own data.
