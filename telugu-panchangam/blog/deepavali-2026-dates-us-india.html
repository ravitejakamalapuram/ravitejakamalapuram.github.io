Why the dates differ between countries

A tithi is a stretch of time that starts and ends at a fixed moment worldwide, but a festival is kept on the day when that tithi is present at a particular point, such as sunrise or moonrise in your own city. The same tithi can therefore fall on different calendar days in Dallas and Hyderabad. It is the same reason Ekadashi fasting days sometimes differ between the two.
